Records

Details and sources

  • Town records list the site as 0 Lighthouse Road, at the corner of Stonewood Drive and Lighthouse Road, Tax Assessor Map 23, Lot 70-B-1 (the hearing notice also writes 70-B1), in the Commercial 1 (C-1) district.
  • A July 14, 2026 Sun Journal listing of June 2026 transactions says Libra Pine Tree Holdings, LLC bought 2.90 acres at 0 Lighthouse Road, Lot 1, from Two Stonewood, LLC.
  • The Oct. 21 agenda described five multifamily condominium buildings with 60 units on a parking lot that is partially wooded. The November hearing notice says "multifamily dwelling units" and does not use the word condominium.
  • The hearing notice names Nathan Edwards with the owner and applicant.
  • The hearing notice describes the review as preliminary subdivision review and site plan review. The legal notice says preliminary major subdivision review and site plan review. The Nov. 18 agenda, posted Nov. 5, lists the application under Old Items, includes a Zoom webinar link, and also lists Fox Ridge, an eight-lot plan off Beech Hill Road. A Nov. 6 legal notice says the public may attend in person or on Zoom.
  • Lighthouse Road was not in OpenStreetMap on Nov. 10, 2026, so the map pin is at Stonewood Drive and the location is approximate.
