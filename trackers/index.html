  • Harpswell · Project tracker

    Mitchell Field's future

    On Oct. 22, the Harpswell Select Board unanimously supported a plan for the Midcoast Council of Governments to hold public workshops before the end of 2026 on the future of George J. Mitchell Field, including two remaining Navy-era buildings. The meeting costs would be covered by a grant. MCOG also offered to submit a formal proposal for a strategic plan in 2027. Workshop dates have not been announced.

    Last updated Nov. 10, 2026

  • Topsham · Project tracker

    Topsham's proposed sewer rate increase

    The Topsham Sewer District proposes raising its quarterly rate from $78 to $82 for each 1,000 cubic feet of water used. The district says the current rates do not cover operations, maintenance, and treatment plant upgrades. Trustees will hear comments at 6 p.m. on Wednesday, Nov. 18, at the district office, then consider final approval at their regular meeting right afterward. If they approve the change, it would take effect Jan. 1, 2027.

    Last updated Nov. 10, 2026
Bath · 1 tracker

  • Bath · Project tracker

    Bath's new elementary school

    RSU 1 is planning a new elementary school to replace Dike-Newell School, which was badly damaged by fire in 2022. The state has said it intends to fund the project through its major capital school construction program. A site has not been chosen. In August the board dropped land behind Bath Middle School and kept the former Dike-Newell site on Wright Drive, plus one other property, under study. A public meeting is set for Dec. 7 at 6 p.m., and a referendum is aimed at June 2027.
