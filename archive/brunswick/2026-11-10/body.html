Data centers · Top story

Council sets a Nov. 16 hearing on extending the data-center moratorium

The June 1 pause on megawatt-scale data centers expires Nov. 28. On Nov. 2 the Council set a Nov. 16 hearing on a further 180-day extension so staff can finish the technical work an outright ban would need, including a grid-impact study still without a consultant. An August workshop had a majority leaning toward a ban.

Housing

Avalon Woods: site walk done, third-party traffic study still ahead

The ~100-unit Avalon Woods / Woodside Road file is still the Planning Board's biggest housing case. On Oct. 27 the board ordered an independent traffic study after Arrowhead Drive neighbors pressed on peak-hour trips; on Nov. 4 it walked the site. Nothing is approved: stormwater, wetlands and road layout are still live.

By the numbers · Housing

Brunswick typical home value sits between Topsham and Freeport

Zillow's Sept. 2026 index puts a typical Brunswick home at about $497,000 (up 2.5% from a year earlier), below Harpswell ($719,000) and Freeport ($685,000), and above Topsham ($469,000) and Bath ($375,000). That figure is a modeled value, not a sale price. State transfer-tax filings put the median recorded Brunswick home sale at $530,000 for Aug.–Oct. 2026, up 11.6% from a year earlier. That comparison leaves out 13 condos at one development that all sold for $325,000 last fall; counting them, the rise looks like 24.9%.

Farmers market

Farmers market wrapped Nov. 7 at Crystal Spring as Dionne Farm's new home takes shape

The BTLT Saturday farmers market's last 2026 day at Crystal Spring Farm was Nov. 7 (Saturdays May 2–Nov. 7). Across Pleasant Hill Road, Phase One at Dionne Farm (begun in August) aims (not a promise) to host the market in 2027 with ~10 more booths, a pavilion, and parking from ~111 spaces to ~140. BTLT puts Phase One at about $2.4 million.

Sports

Brunswick boys soccer wins its 8th Class A state title, 2-0 over Thornton

Brunswick beat Thornton Academy 2-0 on Saturday, Nov. 7, at Kennebunk High School for the Class A boys soccer state title. The first goal came on a header off a deflected free kick with 12:10 left, and a 20-yard left-footed shot added the second with 6:19 left. It is the Dragons' 8th Class A crown and second in five seasons under coach Mark Roma, a year after losing the final to Scarborough. They finish 17-1.

Water quality

Maquoit Bay study lands at Council: nitrogen from the land, not the oyster farm

The Nov. 2 Council packet carried the Maquoit Bay Water Quality Task Force report: nitrogen arrives year-round through groundwater from septics, fertilizer and runoff, with bacteria spikes after rain. On the data, the oyster farm is not the primary cause. Councilors still have to decide whether to keep the task force.

Nature

Before the dam's 2029 relicensing, a Bowdoin biologist makes the fish-passage case

Bowdoin biologist Vladimir Douhovnikoff, science chair of Free the Andro, told a campus audience the Brunswick Dam's 50-year FERC license is up in 2029, a rare window for real fish passage. He favors a "river-runner" half-dam; NOAA and Maine DMR have called the idea feasible. The ask: weigh in on the FERC process now.

This week in history

Auburn Colony fire of Nov. 5, 1899 took all 20 Harpswell cottages

On Nov. 5, 1899, fire swept the Auburn Colony summer retreat in South Harpswell. It started in the cottage nearest the road and took all 20 identical 17-by-30-foot cottages. The dining hall and assembly hall survived. By 1902, fourteen larger shingle-style cottages stood in their place; ten of those still do.
